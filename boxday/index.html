Made for independent freelancers

Many clients. One day.

Choose what fits today. Keep unfinished work within reach. Look back at what you completed.

01

Give each task time today

Write a task, pick a project, and allocate today’s time in 30-minute steps. Project colors keep your client work distinct.

02

See changes before applying

When work moves, check how a time change affects the rest of your plan. Apply only the changes you confirm.

03

Keep the rest without a date

Save unassigned work in Backlog with a note of where you left off. Bring the same task into today when you are ready.

04

See what got done

Review completed and remaining work for today, 7 days, or 30 days. Project charts show planned allocations, not measured working hours.
